# Important — new Pogtown additions (Oct 2026)

Start here if you're new. Three documents define where Pogtown is going:

## 1. `docs/POGTOWN-ROSTER.md` — the cast
Canonical 20-character roster: 10 primary (Popeye, Flip, Felix, Pooh, Holmes, Betty, Alice, Dracula, Frankenstein's Monster, White Rabbit) + 10 wider including originals (Bartholomew III, Martin Lämp, Conspiracy Pigeon, PR1NTR, The Algorithm). Each entry: comedy engine, original (non-cloned) voice direction, GLB starting point, sample joke. Rule: public-domain character, original voice, original design — never a clone.

## 2. `docs/pogtownstyle.md` — the look
Pogtown Puppet Theatre v1: stop-motion-inspired digital puppets, tactile materials, controlled palette, shared rig and expression pack, pose-driven animation, theatrical staging. Plus the Christmas Special (10 concepts) and the personalised-card bridge to OddHobb. This is the brand language — every mesh and scene bows to it.

## 3. `docs/POGTOWN-ENGINE.md` — the contract
How the two repos divide the work: figgsite mines comedy (JokeBlocks, theories, judges, ledger), freaktown performs it (director, stage, voices, audience). Joints table, actor-id namespace, roster pointer.
