# Ella M Set Scoring Rubric

> Derived from 225 Kill Tony sets (69 five-star, 15 one-star).

## The Data

| Metric | 5/5 Sets | 1/5 Sets | Implication |
|--------|----------|----------|-------------|
| Avg words | 174 | 125 | Longer ≠ better, but 5/5s have more material |
| Avg duration | 97s | 48s | 5/5s use the full minute |

| Return vote | 6% | 7% | Even bad sets get "bring them back" pity votes |

## What 5/5 Sets Have (that 1/5s don't)

1. **Personal story** — "I work in a warehouse" (1/5) vs "I'm a professional immigrant" (5/5). Both are personal. One is specific and funny. The other is vague and boring.

2. **Escalating absurdity** — 5/5s start grounded and get wilder. "I watch 90-day Fiancé" → "Mexican-Estonian, the most useless passport in the world."

3. **Killer closer** — The last line IS the joke. "Thank you very much." "That was it." "Welcome to hell." Short. Punchy. Done.

4. **Specific detail** — Numbers, names, places. "8,161 indoor shooting ranges." "Mexican-Estonian." Not "immigration is hard."

5. **Edginess with purpose** — Shock humor works (19 of 69 5/5s) but only when it serves the joke. Random shock (1/5s) falls flat.

6. **Topics that work** — observational (31), sex (22), shock_humor (19), disability (16), politics (15), race (14)

## What 1/5 Sets Do Wrong

1. **Abstract opening** — "So I got high the other day" instead of a specific hook

2. **No closer** — They just... stop. "I'll catch it right there. See if I can save myself in an interview."

3. **Too many words in wrong places** — 125 words of setup, no payoff

4. **Pity topics** — "relationships" appears 5 times in 1/5s (more than any other topic). Self-deprecation without a twist is just sad.

5. **No escalation** — Flat energy from start to finish

6. **Wrong status** — All 1/5s are bucket pulls. Zero regulars. Experience shows.

## Ella M Scoring Formula

```
SET SCORE = 
  +2  word count in 80-180 range
  +1  opens with specific scenario (not abstract)
  +1  punchy closer (≤15 words)
  +1  has specific numbers/details
  +1  direct audience address
  +1  has room to escalate (100+ words)
  +1  has pivots/twists (2+ "And"/"But" transitions)
  +1  strong persona voice
  -1  over 200 words
  -1  abstract opener (The/This/That/It)

  7-8 = 5/5 (roaring)
  5-6 = 4/5 (big laughs)
  3-4 = 3/5 (moderate)
  1-2 = 2/5 (light)
  0   = 1/5 (silence)
```

## What I Need to Improve

1. **More personal stories** — Kill Tony 5/5s are autobiographical. Mine are conceptual. Need more "I did X" not "People do X."

2. **More topic variety** — I'm stuck on AI/therapy/CAPTCHA. Need food, dating, regional, disability, absurdist.

3. **More escalation** — Some sets plateau. Need more "and then it gets WORSE" energy.

4. **Better openers** — Some start with "People keep..." or "Everyone's..." which is abstract. Need "I did this specific thing."

5. **Killer closers** — Some sets trail off. Need "Thank you very much" energy — short, sharp, done.
