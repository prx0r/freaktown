AI Anthropologist: The Final Blueprint & Development RoadmapThesis: A Hierarchically-Trained, Agentic Assembly Line for ComedyThe AI Anthropologist is architected as a hierarchical Mixture of Agents, orchestrated by a master operational agent, BORIS. This system simulates a creative assembly line, where raw material from a "writer's room" is sequentially enriched by specialists in narrative, theory, and performance. BORIS does not create content; it manages the workflow, ensuring each specialist agent contributes its unique value to the final product.The core innovation is the self-improving prompt engine. Each agent in the assembly line maintains and refines its own algorithmic prompt, learning to perfect its specific task through a structured, multi-layered human-in-the-loop training process. This roadmap outlines the definitive plan to build and train this system, layer by layer.1. The Specialist AgentsEach agent is a class with a clear purpose and a self-improving prompt.ComedyWriterAgent:Role: Generates a script in one specific style (e.g., 'dark').Core Prompt: "You are an AI anthropologist with dark humor... Analyze these comments: {comments}. Current context is: {context}. Generate a 150-300 word script."RelationalAgent:Role: Weaves together the five scripts from the writers, adding narrative context.Core Prompt: "You are a master storyteller. Here are five different comedic takes on the same comments: {writer_scripts}. Synthesize them into a single, cohesive draft. Consult your knowledge base to add references to recurring users like 'User_47' and running jokes."TheoryAgent:Role: Adds intellectual framing and updates its knowledge base.Core Prompt: "You are a theorist. Here is a draft script: {relational_draft}. Reframe its core message within the context of your established theories on human behavior (e.g., 'Human Temporal Anxiety'). Extract any new insights for your research."PolishAgent:Role: Adds the final layer of performance art.Core Prompt: "You are a performance director. Here is a final draft: {theory_draft}. Your job is to add the final polish. Insert precise timing markers [PAUSE 0.8s], script visual cues [VISUAL: ...], and, if the wildcard is active, inject this element: {wildcard_instruction}."4.
