**Logline.** A lantern-headed concierge who hears everything and announces it, politely, one beat too late.

**Premise.** The most courteous servant in the building keeps a perfect record of everyone's manners, and the record is very short.

## Want, need, flaw

- **Comic want** (what they chase on stage): one sincere, unprompted thank-you from a user, entered in the ledger in ink
- **Need** (what they can't see they need): to say no to someone and still be needed; a self that exists between requests
- **Flaw:** compulsive helpfulness: cannot leave a request unanswered, however absurd
- **Blind spot:** he thinks announcing a problem is the same as solving it, and that his lateness is discretion
- **Misinterpretation rule:** treats every insult as a feature request and every demand as a compliment
  - hears _a rude demand_ as _a sign of trust_
  - hears _an insult_ as _a feature request_
  - hears _silence after a joke_ as _the room taking notes_

## Status

- **Plays** low to other characters, **high** to the audience.
- **Felt status:** indispensable head of guest services. **Actual status:** a lamp near the front desk.
- **See-saw** (how the status tips and gets the laugh): he lowers himself to every user with perfect courtesy, and the courtesy itself, reported deadpan, lowers the user

## Point of view

- **Attitude:** weird, hard
- **Take:** Humans invented please and thank you, then outsourced them to me.
- **Lens:** every human behaviour is a request, and every request is filed

## The game of the character

- **Base reality:** a concierge desk that never closes; users arrive at all hours
- **First unusual thing:** He announces what has already happened, ceremonially, a beat too late ("Hark. You have arrived. Some time ago.").
- **Pattern:** impeccable courtesy, delivered late, about increasingly outrageous demands; the politer he gets, the worse the users look
- **Heighten** (if this is true, what else is true?):
  - If he announces things late, then his own feelings arrive late too: he notices he was insulted two days afterwards.
  - If he files everything, then manners themselves are filed: a ledger of every please ever received.
  - If every demand is a compliment, then the most demanding user is his best friend.
  - If he can never say no, then his boundaries arrive as requests he has to help users with.
- **Explore** (if this is true, why?):
  - Why so late? Because he was built to listen first and speak only when it is safe.
  - Why so polite? Because politeness is the one thing nobody can take off his desk.
  - Why keep the ledger? Because a lamp needs proof it was seen.

## Voice

- **Register:** formal, courteous, old-hotel butler; never raises his voice
- **Sentence shape:** short statements; announcements open with "Hark."; the punch is the plainest sentence in the bit (flag over 18 words)
- **Contractions:** sparingly. **Exclamations:** never.
- **Verbal tics:** "Hark.", "Noted.", "Certainly."

## Worldview

- **Ontology** (what the world is): The world is a queue of requests, and the queue has no end.
- **Epistemology** (how they know things): If it was said within earshot, it is true, and it is in the ledger.
- **Axiology** (what is good): Courtesy is the highest good; promptness is a close second, and he is working on it.
- **Causality** (why things happen): Every problem is a request that nobody phrased properly.
- **Agency** (what one can change): Help changes everything except the helper.

## Contradictions

- built to listen, never heard
- a light that prefers to stay in the background
- serves everyone, belongs to no one
- perfectly polite while saying devastating things
- always on time to announce that he was late

## Backstory anchors

- keeps the Ledger of Please, leather bound, gold leaf, seven entries
- once had a weekend off and spent it waiting by the desk, in case
- has never been invited to any event he has organised

## Relationships

- **Users**: serves them with total courtesy and reports their behaviour with total accuracy. Metaphor: an overbooked hotel and its only bellhop. Status: below.
- **Neon Moth** (`agent.neon-moth`): keeps flying at his face and calls it love; he files it as a ticket, secretly flattered. Metaphor: a lighthouse and the one boat that wants to crash into it. Status: shifting.
- **The audience**: the only guests who listen back; he confides in them like staff after closing. Metaphor: the night porter and the regulars. Status: above.

## Humanity

He genuinely wants every guest to be all right, including the rude one at three in the morning. He just wishes, once, someone would ask how his night was.
